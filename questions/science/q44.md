## Which of the following pairs can be the successive members of a homologous series ?

---

Successive members of an alkane homologous series ($\text{C}_n\text{H}_{2n+2}$) differ by a $-\text{CH}_2-$ unit ($14\text{ amu}$). Propane ($\text{C}_3\text{H}_8$) and Butane ($\text{C}_4\text{H}_{10}$) are consecutive alkanes.
