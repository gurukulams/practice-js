## In the nuclear reaction $_{6}^{12}\text{X} \rightarrow \alpha\text{ decay} + \text{Y}$, the values of A and Z of daughter nucleus Y are :

---

During an $\alpha$ decay, an alpha particle ($_{2}^{4}\text{He}$) is emitted. Mass number decreases by 4 ($12 - 4 = 8$) and atomic number decreases by 2 ($6 - 2 = 4$). Hence $A = 8, Z = 4$.
