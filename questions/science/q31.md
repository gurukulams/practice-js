## The pH of a solution is 3. Its $[\text{OH}^-]$ concentration is :

---

$\text{pH} + \text{pOH} = 14 \Rightarrow \text{pOH} = 14 - 3 = 11$. Therefore, $[\text{OH}^-] = 10^{-\text{pOH}} = \mathbf{1 \times 10^{-11}\text{ M}}$.
