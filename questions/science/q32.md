## $\text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{OH} + 3\text{O}_2 \rightarrow 2\text{CO}_2 + 3\text{H}_2\text{O}$ is :

---

The complete oxidation/burning of ethanol in excess oxygen to form carbon dioxide and water is called combustion of ethanol.
