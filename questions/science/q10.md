## Match the following :

(1) Sarcoma - (i) Excessive hunger
(2) Carcinoma - (ii) Excessive thirst
(3) Polydipsia - (iii) Connective tissue cancer
(4) Polyphagia - (iv) Stomach cancer

---

Sarcoma arises from connective and muscular tissue (iii). Carcinoma arises from epithelial tissue like stomach cancer (iv). Polydipsia is excessive thirst (ii). Polyphagia is excessive hunger (i).
