## If the earth shrinks to 50% of its real radius its mass remaining the same, the weight of a body on earth will :

---

Acceleration due to gravity $g = \frac{GM}{R^2}$. If radius becomes $R' = R/2$, then $g' = \frac{GM}{(R/2)^2} = 4g$. The new weight is $4W$. The percentage increase is $\frac{4W - W}{W} \times 100 = \mathbf{300\%}$.
