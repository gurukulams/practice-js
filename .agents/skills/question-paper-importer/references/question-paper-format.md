# Tamil Nadu SSLC Science Question Paper Structure

The Tamil Nadu State Board Class 10 (SSLC) Science question paper consists of 75 total marks distributed across four parts:

## Marks Breakdown

| Part | Marks per Question | Questions | Total Marks | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **PART - I** | 1 Mark | Q01 – Q12 (Answer all 12) | 12 Marks | Multiple Choice Questions with 4 alternatives (A, B, C, D) |
| **PART - II** | 2 Marks | Q13 – Q22 (Answer any 7, Q22 is compulsory) | 14 Marks | Short answer questions covering Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Computer Science |
| **PART - III** | 4 Marks | Q23 – Q32 (Answer any 7, Q32 is compulsory) | 28 Marks | Medium answer questions, derivations, differences, and numerical problems |
| **PART - IV** | 7 Marks | Q33 – Q35 (Answer all 3, internal choice A or B) | 21 Marks | Detailed descriptive questions covering Physics (Q33), Chemistry (Q34), and Biology (Q35) |

**Total:** 12 + 14 + 28 + 21 = **75 Marks** (Time allowed: 3.00 Hours).
