## The eye defect 'Presbyopia' can be corrected by :

---

Presbyopia is old-age hypermetropia caused by weakening of ciliary muscles and decreasing flexibility of the eye lens. It is corrected using bifocal lenses (upper portion concave, lower portion convex).
