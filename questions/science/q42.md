## Solubility of NaCl in 100 ml water is 36 g. If 25 g of salt is dissolved in 100 ml of water, how much more salt is needed to saturate the solution ?

---

Amount of solute required for saturation = Solubility value ($36\text{ g}$) - Dissolved amount ($25\text{ g}$) = $36 - 25 = \mathbf{11\text{ g}}$.
