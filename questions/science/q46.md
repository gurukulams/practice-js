## The loss of one or more Chromosome in a ploidy is called __________.

---

Aneuploidy is the condition where an organism has a chromosome number that is not an exact multiple of the basic haploid set due to loss or gain of chromosomes (e.g., monosomy $2n-1$, nullisomy $2n-2$).
