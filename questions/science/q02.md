## In which of the following reaction, the mass number decreases by four of the daughter nucleus ?

---

In an $\alpha$ (alpha) decay, the daughter nucleus has a mass number that decreases by 4 and an atomic number that decreases by 2 ($_{Z}^{A}X \rightarrow _{Z-2}^{A-4}Y + _{2}^{4}\text{He}$).
